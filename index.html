安心日记 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 7 日

安心日记（以下简称「本 App」）由 Anxin Labs 开发。我们很重视您和家人的隐私。这份政策说明本 App 会处理哪些资料、资料存放在哪里，以及您可以怎样管理它们。

一、资料只存在您的手机里

本 App 不需要注册账号，也没有我们自己的服务器。您在本 App 里记录的所有内容，都只存放在这支手机上本 App 专用的储存空间里，我们看不到，也不会收集、出售或分享。这些内容包括：

  • 吃喝记录：您拍的照片和写的文字
  • 药的资料：药名、份量、提醒时间、药盒照片，以及每次吃药或跳过的记录
  • 血压记录：高压、低压、心跳和量的时间
  • App 的设置，例如字体大小和语言

二、相机

本 App 只在您按下拍照时使用相机，用来拍吃的喝的、药盒，或血压计。

拍血压计时，照片只在手机上读取数字、帮您填写，读完就删除，不会保存。

三、通知和闹钟

本 App 使用通知和准时闹钟，在您设定的时间提醒吃药。提醒都在手机上安排，不经过网络。

四、Google ML Kit（读取血压计上的数字）

本 App 使用 Google 的 ML Kit 文字识别功能读取血压计上的数字。识别完全在手机上进行，照片和识别出的数字都不会传送出去。

但 ML Kit 可能会把匿名的诊断和使用资料传给 Google，例如手机型号、系统版本、App 版本，以及识别的速度和错误情况，用来改善这项服务。这些资料不包含您的照片或记录。详情请参考 Google 的隐私权政策：https://policies.google.com/privacy

五、手机备份

如果您在手机上开启了 Google 备份，Android 系统可能会把本 App 的资料（包括记录和照片）备份到您自己的 Google 账号，方便换手机时还原。这项备份由 Google 依照其隐私权政策处理，我们无法存取。您可以在手机的「设置 → 系统 → 备份」里关闭。

六、删除资料

  • 在本 App 里，可以删除单条吃喝记录和血压记录。
  • 「不再吃这个药」会停止提醒，但会保留以前的吃药记录，方便日后查看。
  • 卸载本 App，或在手机设置里清除本 App 的资料，会删除所有记录和照片。

七、健康资料与医疗声明

本 App 的血压分级和建议仅供日常记录参考，不能代替医生的诊断和治疗。身体不舒服请看医生。

八、儿童

本 App 是为成人和家中长辈设计的，并非针对 13 岁以下儿童。

九、政策更新

如果这份政策有更改，我们会更新本页面并修改上面的「最后更新」日期。

十、联络我们

如对本政策有任何问题，请通过本 App 在 Google Play 页面上的开发者邮箱联系我们。

CareDiary (安心日记) Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

CareDiary ("the app") is developed by Anxin Labs. We take your and your family's privacy seriously. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, and how you can manage it.

1. Your information stays on your phone

The app needs no account and has no servers of ours. Everything you record is stored only on this phone, in storage private to the app. We cannot see it, and we do not collect, sell or share it. This includes:

  • Food and drink records: the photos you take and the notes you write
  • Medicines: names, doses, reminder times, photos of the boxes, and each dose taken or skipped
  • Blood pressure: the high and low numbers, pulse and time of each reading
  • App settings, such as text size and language

2. Camera

The app uses the camera only when you choose to take a photo: of food and drink, a medicine box, or a blood pressure monitor.

A photo of a blood pressure monitor is read on the phone to fill in the numbers, then deleted. It is never kept.

3. Notifications and alarms

The app uses notifications and exact alarms to remind you to take medicine at the times you set. Reminders are scheduled on the phone and do not use the internet.

4. Google ML Kit (reading the blood pressure monitor)

The app uses Google's ML Kit text recognition to read the numbers on a blood pressure monitor. Recognition runs entirely on the phone; the photo and the numbers read from it are not sent anywhere.

ML Kit may send Google anonymous diagnostic and usage information, such as the phone model, Android version, app version, and the speed and errors of recognition, to improve the service. This does not include your photos or records. See Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Phone backup

If Google backup is turned on for your phone, Android may back up the app's data (including records and photos) to your own Google account, so it can be restored on a new phone. This backup is handled by Google under its privacy policy, and we cannot access it. You can turn it off in your phone's Settings → System → Backup.

6. Deleting your information

  • In the app, you can delete individual food and drink records and blood pressure readings.
  • "Stop taking this medicine" stops its reminders but keeps the past dose records so they can still be looked back on.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in the phone's settings, deletes all records and photos.

7. Health information and medical disclaimer

The app's blood pressure ratings and advice are for everyday record-keeping only and are not a substitute for a doctor's diagnosis or treatment. If you feel unwell, please see a doctor.

8. Children

The app is designed for adults and older family members, and is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at the developer email shown on the app's Google Play page.
